## 2025-03-09 - Asyncio Gather Loop Refactoring Note
**Learning:** When refactoring sequential async calls inside a `try...except` block into concurrent `asyncio.gather(*tasks, return_exceptions=True)` execution, always iterate over the `results` and log or handle `Exception` objects directly. Swallowing them silently removes visibility into critical partial failures (e.g., individual Firestore notification insertions failing).
**Action:** Consistently pair `return_exceptions=True` with explicit result iteration (`isinstance(res, Exception)`) rather than assuming `asyncio.gather` acts as a silent success aggregator.
