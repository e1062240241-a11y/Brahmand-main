# 49 Shakti Peethas — Complete Guide

**Temple Timings, Aarti Schedule, Prohibited Items & Dress Code**

## 1. Kamakhya Temple — Guwahati, Assam

**Significance:** Sati's Yoni fell here. One of the most revered Shakti Peethas.

**Temple Timing**
- **Opening Time:** 8:00 AM (after morning puja); temple activities begin at 5:30 AM with Snana
- **Closing Time:** 5:15 PM (door closes for the night); Aarti at 7:30 PM
- **Darshan Hours:** 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM; 2:30 PM – 5:15 PM (closes for cooked offerings 1:00–2:30 PM)

**Aarti / Ritual Schedule**
- **Snana of the Pithasthana:** 5:30 AM
- **Nitya Puja:** 6:00 AM
- **Temple door opens for devotees:** 8:00 AM
- **Cooked offerings (door closed):** 1:00 PM
- **Temple reopens:** 2:30 PM
- **Closing of temple door:** 5:15 PM (sunset)
